Privacy Policy

Step4Progress Ascend · Last updated 30 October 2026

Step4Progress ("we", "us") runs the Step4Progress Ascend app and website, which help students prepare for studying abroad: lessons, IELTS practice, full mock tests, an AI speaking partner called Spark, and tools for visa and university preparation. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your mobile phone number (used to sign in with a one-time code), your name, and the profile details you choose to enter (for example target course, university, destination country, academic background).
  • Your study activity: lesson progress, vocabulary progress, mock test answers and scores, writing you submit for marking, and speaking practice. Speaking is turned into text on your device or by your phone's speech service; we store the text of your answers, not audio recordings.
  • Documents and notes you choose to upload or type, such as an essay for review.
  • Purchase information: which plan you bought and the store's transaction number. We never see your card or UPI details; payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the service, such as error logs.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, show your progress, and give you the plan you bought.
  • To generate and mark practice material and to reply in Spark. Your text answers are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the reply or feedback.
  • To send you a verification code by SMS.
  • To improve and secure the service and to answer your questions.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database and sign-in hosting)
  • Anthropic (AI replies, feedback and marking)
  • An SMS provider (verification codes)
  • RevenueCat, Google Play and Apple (purchases)

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it in countries outside your own.

Counselling

If you are also a Step4Progress counselling client, our counsellors may see your progress and the details you shared so they can support your application.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (Profile, or the "Delete my account" link at the bottom of any page). Deleting removes your account, progress, profile and saved work. We keep only the record of purchases that we need for accounting.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in the app, or email us and we will do it for you.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction by email.
  • Turn off microphone access in your phone settings; you can still type to Spark.

Children

The app is meant for students aged 16 and over. If you are under 16 please use it only with a parent or guardian, and contact us if you think a child has given us data without permission.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access is restricted by account. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone safe.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
